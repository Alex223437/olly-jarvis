Privacy policy

Olly Jarvis · last updated November 2026

Olly Jarvis is a private, self-hosted assistant. It is used only by the person who runs it, with their own Google account. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

When the owner connects their Google account, Olly Jarvis can read and manage that account's Gmail messages, Google Calendar events, Google Tasks, Google Drive files, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms and Contacts.

How the data is used

Only to carry out the owner's own requests, for example checking new mail, adding a calendar event or creating a document. Nothing is sent to other people unless the owner explicitly asks for it. The data is not sold, not used for advertising and not used by Olly Jarvis to train AI models.

To answer a request, the relevant content may be processed by the AI model providers the assistant runs on (Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepSeek), under their own terms.

Storage

OAuth tokens and the assistant's notes are stored on the owner's own server, readable only by the assistant's service account. The owner can delete them at any time.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
